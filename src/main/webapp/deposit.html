Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a deposit take?

Cash, UPI and Net Banking deposits are usually credited instantly. Cheque deposits may take 1–2 working days.

Is there any deposit limit?

Limits depend on your account type and payment method. Please refer to SecureBank's deposit policy.

Can I cancel a deposit?

Once a successful deposit is processed, it cannot be cancelled. Contact customer support for assistance.
