Frequently Asked Questions

Which transfer method should I use?

Use IMPS or UPI for instant transfers, NEFT for standard bank transfers, and RTGS for high-value transactions.

Can I cancel a transfer?

Instant transfers cannot be cancelled once processed. Pending NEFT or RTGS transfers may be cancelled before processing.

Is there a daily transfer limit?

Yes. Your daily transfer limit is ₹5,00,000. The limit may vary depending on your account type.
